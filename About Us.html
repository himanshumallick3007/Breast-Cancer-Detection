About Us

Welcome to Mammography AI, a project dedicated to early detection of breast cancer using advanced image processing and AI technologies. Our mission is to provide an accessible, AI-powered solution for analyzing mammograms and assisting in early diagnosis, potentially saving lives through timely intervention.
